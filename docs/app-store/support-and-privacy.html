MB Music Tools

A free tuner and metronome for band practice, for iPhone and iPad.

Support

For help or feedback, use the project's GitHub issue tracker. Tell us which tool you were using, what happened, and your app and iOS versions. Issues are public. Keep private information out of your report.

The tuner needs microphone access. You can change it in iOS Settings. Choose Concert, B-flat, E-flat, or F to match your instrument. The tuner uses A = 440 Hz. The metronome supports 30–300 BPM, tap tempo, custom meters, and six click rhythms.

Privacy policy

Updated October 9, 2026. This policy describes MB Music Tools for iPhone and iPad. The maintainers of the project are responsible for the app.

Microphone audio

The tuner processes microphone audio on your device to measure pitch. Audio is not recorded, saved, or uploaded. Microphone access stops when you leave the tuner or put the app in the background. You can decline or revoke microphone access and continue using the metronome.

Saved settings

Tempo, time signature, click rhythm, transposition, and appearance preferences are saved on your device. Delete the app to remove these preferences. Offloading preserves app data. Device backups may retain settings according to your Apple backup settings.

Data collection

MB Music Tools has no accounts, advertising, purchases, analytics SDKs, or tracking. We do not collect your microphone audio or practice activity.

External links and feedback

Opening GitHub links connects to GitHub, which handles information under its own privacy policy. If you send us feedback, we receive the information you choose to share and use it to answer your request and investigate problems. GitHub issues are public. For a sensitive request, ask for a private contact method without posting the sensitive information.

Apple diagnostics and beta testing

Apple may share diagnostic reports according to your device settings and Apple's policies. TestFlight beta testing may provide us with crash reports, usage information, device details, and feedback you send. See TestFlight and Privacy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions or requests, use the issue tracker. We will update this policy when the app's handling of information changes.
