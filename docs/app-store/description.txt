MB Music Tools is a free tuner and metronome for band practice.

Tune in concert pitch, or choose B-flat, E-flat, or F to see the written note for your instrument. The tuner shows how far a note is sharp or flat and gives a gentle haptic when you reach the center. It uses A = 440 Hz and processes microphone audio on your device.

Set the metronome from 30 to 300 BPM, tap along to find a tempo, or drag the tempo controls to adjust it. Choose a common time signature or make your own. Whole, half, quarter, eighth, triplet, and sixteenth-note clicks help you work through different rhythms. Playback continues when you lock your screen.

Your tempo, meter, click rhythm, transposition, and appearance settings are remembered between practices. Use automatic, light, or dark appearance on iPhone and iPad.

Every feature is free. There are no ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or accounts. The app is open source, and microphone audio is never saved or uploaded.
